Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

LaraPost ("the Service") is a social-media scheduling and publishing tool operated by an individual developer ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have about that information.

Information we collect

  • Meta account information — when you connect a Facebook Page or Instagram Business account, Meta's OAuth flow provides us with basic profile information (such as your name and user ID) and the list of Pages and Instagram accounts you manage.
  • Page and account details — the name, ID, and username of each Page or Instagram account you connect, plus the access tokens required to publish content on your behalf.
  • Content you publish — the captions and media (images and videos) you schedule or publish through the Service, along with your posting schedule and preferences.

How we use the information

  • To publish content to the Pages and Instagram accounts you explicitly connect and authorize.
  • To store your credentials and schedule so that posting runs automatically.
  • To operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the Service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except with Meta (Facebook) as necessary to perform publishing through their official API.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored on a server controlled by the operator (a MySQL database).
  • Access tokens and credentials are encrypted at rest.
  • Tokens and credentials are never exposed in any API response or log.

Data retention

We retain your data while you maintain an active connection to the Service. You may disconnect any Page or account at any time.

Your rights and data deletion

You may request that we delete the personal information we hold about you. See our Data Deletion Instructions or contact us at support@larapost.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@larapost.com.
