User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 30, 2026

LaraPost stores the data needed to publish content to your connected Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts. You may request deletion of this data at any time.

What data is deleted

  • Meta account profile information (name, user ID).
  • Connected Page and Instagram account details and access tokens.
  • Your posting schedules and preferences.

How to request deletion

Send an email to support@larapost.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Include the Facebook account or Page you used to connect to the Service so we can locate your data.

We will process your request and remove the associated data within 30 days of receiving it. We will also disconnect any connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts from the Service.

Disconnecting an account yourself

You can also disconnect any Page or Instagram account directly from the Service at any time, which revokes the Service's access to publish on your behalf.
